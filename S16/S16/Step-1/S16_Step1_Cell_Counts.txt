COUNTING BASIS
Each source image was inspected independently. Ten visible, approximately
vertical scribed boundaries and ten approximately horizontal scribed
boundaries enclose 9 x 9 = 81 cells. Coating margins outside the outermost
cuts and cut extensions beyond the enclosed grid are not counted as cells.
The four images show the same physical grid under different illumination;
the counts describe each image, not 324 separate physical cells.

NUMBERING
The first enclosed cell at the upper left is 0. Numbers increase left to
right, then continue on the next row downward. Rows contain:
0-8, 9-17, 18-26, 27-35, 36-44, 45-53, 54-62, 63-71, 72-80.

OVERLAYS AND GEOMETRY
Green boundaries follow the local bends in the scribed cuts. Individual
boundaries are traced separately in all four images, rather than placing a
straight rectangular grid over the sample. Brief obscured sections follow
the adjacent visible cut course; their exact centreline is an estimate.
All images retain their original 1440 x 1080 pixel size and orientation.
No rotation, resampling or geometric registration was applied.
Light-yellow fill: RGB (255, 246, 120), opacity 66/255 = 25.9%.
The underlying surface remains visible. Green line width is 3 pixels.

DISPLAY AND ORIGINAL DATA
The sources are 16-bit grayscale TIFF files. PNG deliverables use a
per-image display window (raw-value percentiles 0.1-99.5) and a gamma
exponent of 0.8 so that the coating and cuts remain visible under the
different lighting conditions. This affects only display brightness.
Original TIFF files are not modified. Raw-to-display parameters and source
SHA-256 values are recorded in Reusable_Grid_Data/S16_Step1_Grid_Definitions.json.
Display PNGs are not suitable for quantitative intensity comparison.

FILES
For each image:
  *_Green_Boundaries.png : green cut boundaries, no yellow fill or numbers.
  *_Yellow_Cells.png     : transparent light-yellow cells with green boundaries.
  *_Numbered_0-80.png    : the yellow/green view with individual cell numbers.

Additional files:
  S16_Step1_Numbered_Overview.jpg : four numbered views for quick inspection.
  Reusable_Grid_Data/*_Transparent_Grid_Overlay.png : RGBA overlay at original size.
  Reusable_Grid_Data/*_Cell_IDs_1-81.png : 16-bit pixel labels; 0 is outside,
      1-81 correspond to displayed cells 0-80 respectively.
  Reusable_Grid_Data/S16_Step1_Grid_Definitions.json : curved boundary coordinates,
      individual cell polygons, cell centroids and source identity.

Step 1 identifies cells only. No adhesion loss, retention, or severity
is calculated from these pre-tape images.

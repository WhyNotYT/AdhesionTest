WHAT THE PERCENTAGES MEAN
Assessed area = pixels inside the original cell grid after excluding original tool-scraped grooves and their pre-existing dark walls/chips, with a one-pixel registration margin.
Estimated peeled area = newly changed coating pixels selected by the paired-image detector outside those excluded grooves.
Peeled AM estimate (%) = selected blue-mask pixels / assessed area pixels x 100.
Intact AM (%) = 100 - peeled AM estimate (%). This is an optical area-retention proxy, not a mass fraction or a mechanical adhesion-strength measurement.

INTERPRETATION AND UNCERTAINTY
The main high-sensitivity estimate includes two classes: new bright foil-like exposure and new strongly darker edge/pit damage relative to the aligned Squared reference.
The dark class is treated as inferred coating loss in the main estimate. Shadows, altered surface relief, or reflectance can also produce dark changes, so actual exposed-foil area is not uniquely identifiable from these grayscale views.
The bright-only subset is reported separately for comparison. It too is an optical classification, not chemical confirmation of aluminium.
All counted pixels are shown in light blue in Loss_Blue and Combined_Overlay images. Bright and dark component masks are also provided separately.
A damaged cell contains at least one accepted changed cluster. No detected loss would mean no accepted cluster, not proof of absolute zero damage below the image detection limit.
Every cell contains detected changes at the selected thresholds; therefore all 81 cells are orange, with zero intact-class and zero excluded-class cells. Separate Yellow_Cells files show the complete requested light-yellow grid.

COLOURS
Green = curved cell boundaries. Light yellow = no detected damage. Orange = detected damage. Red = excluded whole cell, if any. Light blue = pixels included in the loss estimate.
Excluded_Grooves_Overlay shows excluded existing scrape areas in red. This diagnostic red groove area does not mean the surrounding whole cells were excluded.

ALIGNMENT AND ORIGINAL SCRAPE EXCLUSION
Each Squared image was matched only with its equally numbered Taped counterpart.
The source photographs have the same upright orientation. Small translations, scale changes, shear, and in-plane rotation were estimated from surviving surface texture using affine registration.
Each transform used 72 accepted cell-interior patch correspondences. Median residuals are approximately 0.33-0.39 pixels; 95th-percentile residuals are approximately 0.85-0.91 pixels.
Only the reference was resampled into the original Taped image coordinates. All individual output images remain 1440 x 1080 pixels.
Groove exclusion starts with the traced reference groove centre within two pixels, then includes connected pre-existing dark groove walls within 16 pixels where the reference is less than 0.78 of local background. A one-pixel margin covers registration/interpolation uncertainty.
New changes outside the pre-existing scrape mask can be counted even when adjacent to the old groove. Entire grid-edge strips were not blanket-excluded.

HIGH-SENSITIVITY DETECTOR
Raw 16-bit-container TIFF intensities were used for calculations. PNG contrast adjustment is for display only.
For comparison, the aligned raw images were smoothed with a 0.9-pixel Gaussian to suppress isolated sensor noise.
A robust spatial photometric fit from surviving cell interiors compensates for exposure and illumination gradients. Residual noise was estimated separately in each cell interior.
Main threshold: changed-cluster seed exceeds +/-4 local residual-noise units, with observed/predicted intensity ratio above 1.40 (bright) or below 0.65 (dark).
Clusters grow only through observed changed pixels exceeding +/-2.5 noise units and ratios above 1.18 or below 0.82. Accepted connected clusters contain at least 8 pixels.
Strict comparison uses seed 5, grow 3.2, ratios 1.55/0.55, and a 12-pixel minimum. Relaxed comparison uses seed 3.5, grow 2.0, ratios 1.30/0.72, and a 6-pixel minimum.
The strict mask is a subset and the relaxed mask a superset of the main mask. Percentages are recalculated from their selected pixels, not multiplied by a sensitivity factor.

REPEAT-VIEW CONTROL
An unpeeled repeat-view comparison (Squared [1] versus Squared [2]) yielded zero accepted changed pixels at all three settings. This checks repeat-view noise rejection; it does not rule out all illumination or shadow errors after peeling.

DELIVERED FILES
Per image: original display, green boundaries, yellow cells, numbering, cell status, light-blue loss, combined overlay, transparent combined layer, paired reference comparison, registration diagnostic, original-groove exclusion diagnostic, and binary/ID masks.

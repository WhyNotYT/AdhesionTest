AREA DEFINITION
Retained AM means eligible original coated area with no detected new loss.
Eligible original AM excludes the original tool-scraped cut bands, a small
registration safety margin, pre-existing bright foil candidates and the
area outside the enclosed grid. These exclusions apply to numerator and
denominator. No whole cells were excluded.
Loss (%) = 100 * new loss pixels / eligible original AM pixels.
Retained (%) = 100 - loss (%).
A damaged cell has at least one retained connected loss component.
An intact cell has no loss detected by this setting. This does not prove
physical zero damage. Severity is expressed as area loss, without assigning
an ASTM or other standardized test grade.

REGISTRATION AND RAW SIGNAL
Each Squared image was paired only with its matching Taped image. An affine
transform fitted to stable texture/cut matches corrects translation, small
rotation and scale. The native Taped orientation and dimensions are kept.
Measurements use original 16-bit containers containing approximately
10-bit signal. A 0.6-pixel Gaussian smoothing suppresses sensor noise.
A robust second-order spatial fit to paired log-intensity ratios corrects
exposure/illumination differences. It is fitted from cell interiors away
from cuts. The fixed sqrt(raw/1023) display mapping is not used to measure
loss. The aligned reference display has exposure correction applied.

ORIGINAL SCRAPE EXCLUSION
The Step-1 curved cut centerlines are retained. Connected dark or bright
original groove pixels near the cut centerlines extend the exclusion
locally. A two-pixel margin covers alignment uncertainty. Bright original
interior foil candidates are excluded separately. The exact excluded-pixel
masks are provided. New bright material-loss regions extending beyond the
original excluded bands can be included. The safety margin can omit a
small amount of loss immediately beside a pre-existing cut.

HIGH-SENSITIVITY DETECTION
A counted loss pixel must satisfy ALL of the following:
1. It lies in the eligible original-AM region.
2. Its local Taped intensity is at least 1.5 times the fitted local coating
   background.
3. Its paired log-intensity increase exceeds max(0.22, 2.0 * sigma), after
   exposure correction. Sigma is estimated robustly from negative residuals
   in stable cell interiors.
4. It belongs to an eight-connected component of at least three pixels.
Blue pixels exactly match the measured loss masks. They are not expanded
or dilated for display.

THRESHOLD COMPARISON
These operational settings show threshold dependence. They are not a
statistical confidence interval or proof of material identity.
High: local brightness ratio 1.5; paired change 2.0 sigma (primary).
Moderate: ratio 1.7; paired change 2.5 sigma.
Conservative: ratio 2.0; paired change 3.0 sigma.

INTERPRETATION
The blue regions are high-sensitivity optical candidates for new exposed
substrate or reduced coating coverage. Fine bright texture can also change
with illumination or surface roughness. Large bright changes next to cuts
are clearer than tiny isolated clusters. The estimate describes visible
area retention in this image field, not adhesion force, retained mass,
coating thickness or a standardized classification.
The four views repeat one specimen. They are not four independent samples.
Their arithmetic mean summarizes repeated-view estimates. No precision
beyond the image analysis and chosen thresholds is implied.

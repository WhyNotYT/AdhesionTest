S13 STEP-2 UPDATED RESULTS: 5.0x SENSITIVITY

OPERATIONAL DEFINITION
Sensitivity factor 5.0 is implemented by dividing BOTH original log-contrast
thresholds by 5.0. This is a detector-setting change. The area percentages
are recalculated by classifying the paired pixels at the new thresholds.
The detected area is not expected to increase by exactly five times.

Original brightness ratio 1.5 becomes 1.5^(1/5) = 1.0844717712.
Original paired thresholds max(0.22, 2*sigma) become that full value / 5.0.
For these four images, the updated paired threshold equals 0.4*sigma.
The original 3-pixel, 8-connected component requirement remains in place.

IMPORTANT INTERPRETATION
The updated thresholds are below the measured brightness variation. They
admit faint coating texture, lighting differences and residual alignment
effects. Blue marks candidate material-loss pixels at this sensitivity.
A percentage calculated from these pixels is an optical sensitivity
estimate. The images do not confirm that every new blue pixel is bare Al.
The physical sample has not changed between the two calculations.

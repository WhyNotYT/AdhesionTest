Each image independently shows ten vertical scribe cuts and ten horizontal
scribe cuts, enclosing nine columns and nine rows (9 x 9 = 81 cells).
These are four views of the same grid: 81 physical cells, 324 cell-view entries.

Numbering starts at the upper-left ENCLOSED cell, from left to right, then
continues on the next row. Top row: 0-8; bottom row: 72-80.
The surrounding material beyond the outermost cuts and the extended cut
strokes outside that enclosed lattice are not additional bounded grid cells.

The source images were examined independently; no previous sample count or
previous annotation was used. Cut paths were traced separately in each source.
Faint and discontinuous sections are connected by interpolation, guided by
visible adjacent portions. Local boundary placement is approximate where the
original cut is not clearly visible; a straight uniform grid was not imposed.
No image required a 90-degree/180-degree correction. Each output retains its
original orientation and 1440 x 1080 pixel dimensions.

columns 2 and 3, counting columns from 0), is faint/discontinuous in all four
exposures. It is supported by visible
segments and the repeating cut lattice; its gaps have been interpolated.
